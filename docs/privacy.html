Privacy Policy — EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER

1. Core Privacy Principle: Zero Data Collection

We believe your personal audio recordings, voice notes, and listening habits are strictly private. EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER is a local-first application. We do NOT collect, harvest, upload, transmit, analyze, or sell any of your audio files, voice recordings, personal identifiers, or device data.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No Audio or Voice Uploads: All audio recordings and voice memos remain exclusively on your device's internal storage or SD card. We do not operate remote servers to harvest or store audio.
  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins.
  • No Audio Biometrics: We do not perform voice recognition, voice printing, or identity profiling.
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER contains zero ads, no Google AdMob, no Facebook SDK, and no commercial tracking libraries.
  • No Location Tracking: The app does not access, record, or track your GPS coordinates.

3. Android Runtime Permissions & Operational Usage

Permission Android Identifier Operational Purpose in EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER
Microphone RECORD_AUDIO Mandatory to capture high-clarity voice memos, lectures, and speech notes when you double-tap. Saved locally on your device.
Media Audio / Storage READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Allows the app to read, play, index, and organize audio files on your device and save new recordings.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Required by Android for the foreground service notification so recording and playback continue when the screen is dark.
Vibration VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic feedback pulses on taps and swipes so blind and visually impaired users can operate by touch.
Wake Lock WAKE_LOCK Prevents the phone CPU from entering sleep during active recording or playback sessions.

4. Text-To-Speech (TTS) Processing

Spoken announcements (file names, folder names, battery level, timestamps) are generated strictly on-device using your Android system's default Text-to-Speech engine. No speech queries or audio synthesized by TTS are transmitted to any proprietary external servers.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). The app is fully compliant with COPPA and global child protection standards.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You have 100% control over your data. Audio files can be permanently erased at any time using the 4-finger swipe right gesture within the app or using any standard Android file manager. Uninstalling the app removes all application configuration caches.
