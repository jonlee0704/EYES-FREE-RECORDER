## 1. Audio Recording, Eavesdropping & Wiretapping Laws

### 1.1 The Legal Risk
Audio recording technology is subject to strict criminal and civil penalties under federal, state, and international wiretapping and eavesdropping statutes. In many jurisdictions, recording a conversation without the knowledge and consent of all participating individuals constitutes a felony or misdemeanor offense.

### 1.2 Multi-Jurisdiction Consent Breakdown
- **United States Federal Law (18 U.S.C. § 2511)**: Requires "one-party consent," meaning a person may record a conversation to which they are a party, or where one party has given prior consent.
- **Two-Party / All-Party Consent States**: At least twelve (12) U.S. states strictly mandate that **all participants** in a conversation must explicitly consent before any recording may take place:
  - **California** (Cal. Penal Code § 632)
  - **Florida** (Fla. Stat. § 934.03)
  - **Illinois** (720 ILCS 5/14-1 et seq.)
  - **Massachusetts** (Mass. Gen. Laws ch. 272 § 99)
  - **Maryland** (Md. Code Ann., Cts. & Jud. Proc. § 10-402)
  - **Pennsylvania** (18 Pa. Cons. Stat. § 5703)
  - **Connecticut, Montana, New Hampshire, Washington, Nevada, and Delaware** (subject to varying statutory interpretations).
- **International Jurisdictions**:
  - **European Union / UK**: Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), a person's voice constitutes biometric personal data. Recording conversations in commercial, organizational, or public settings without a lawful basis or explicit consent violates data protection laws.
  - **Canada**: Section 184 of the Criminal Code enforces one-party consent for private communications.
  - **Australia**: State surveillance device acts (e.g. Surveillance Devices Act 2007 NSW) strictly prohibit recording private conversations without all-party consent.

### 1.3 Developer Immunity & User Sole Responsibility
1. **Tool Provider Status**: EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER is a neutral software utility capable of legitimate, lawful use (personal voice memos, dictated notes, music practice, authorized classroom lectures).
2. **No Affirmative Control**: Developer has no physical control over how, where, or when an end user triggers the recording function.
3. **Acknowledgment by User**: By using EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER, the user explicitly acknowledges that:
   - They assume **sole legal and financial responsibility** for verifying and complying with all applicable recording, eavesdropping, wiretapping, and privacy statutes in their jurisdiction.
   - Developer shall not be joined as a party to, nor held liable for, any civil lawsuit, criminal indictment, fine, or dispute arising out of the user's surreptitious or unlawful audio recording of any individual.

## 4. Operating System Interruptions & Battery Management

### 4.1 Android Task Killing & Doze Mode
Modern Android operating systems (Android 10 through Android 15+) employ aggressive background battery management policies ("Doze Mode", "App Standby Buckets", and OEM battery killers like Samsung Device Care or Xiaomi MIUI cleaners):
- If the operating system determines that system resources or battery levels are low, Android may forcibly terminate background recording services without warning.
- Incoming cellular calls, alarms, or low-battery shutdowns can interrupt an active audio stream.

### 4.2 Disclaimer of Recording Continuity
- Developer does **NOT** guarantee that any recording session will continue uninterrupted for any specific duration.
- The user is responsible for ensuring the device is sufficiently charged, disabling aggressive OS battery optimizations for EYES-FREE VOICE RECORDER if long-duration recording is required, and testing recording continuity on their specific device hardware.
